## Design Context

### Users
The primary users are potential collaborators, employers, and fellow developers. They are looking for technical competence but want to be "moved" by the experience. The context is a professional yet deeply personal portfolio visit.

### Brand Personality
1. **Zen Garden**: Calm, intentional, nature-rooted. A Japanese sensibility of restraint and purpose.
2. **Typographic**: The design is carried by words, spacing, and rhythm — not effects.
3. **Narrative**: The page unfolds like a scroll, each section a stone in a garden path.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Wabi-sabi zen garden. Warm paper/ink tones, generous whitespace, poetic copy.
- **References**: Japanese editorial design, karesansui (dry landscape gardens), ma (purposeful emptiness).
- **Anti-References**: Generic tech portfolios, glassmorphism, neon-on-dark "AI" aesthetics, aggressively techy vibes.
- **Constraints**: **NO GLASS**. Shaders should be calm, not aggressive. Typography and story are primary.

### Design Principles
1. **Ma (間)**: Purposeful emptiness. Whitespace is a design element, not leftover space.
2. **Warm Palette**: Both themes use warm, natural tones — ink-on-paper in dark, earth-on-parchment in light.
3. **Typography First**: The voice carries the design. Copy should feel poetic, minimal, and intentional.
4. **Organic Motion**: Spring physics over mechanical easing. Transitions should feel like breathing.
5. **Readable Always**: No gray-on-gray. Every text element must be clearly readable in both themes.
